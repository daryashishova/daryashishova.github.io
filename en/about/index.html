About me

Hello, I’m Dasha. I create original animated films and animation for documentaries, run workshops and creative labs in animation and sketching for children and adults, travel with sketchbooks, and teach dance drawing.

I love the color red, especially in harmony with all the other colors. I paint in oils, draw sketches and create graphic series.
